# Six Words, One Story

A complete story told in exactly six words: "The letter arrived after the funeral."

Someone wrote a letter, and by the time it arrived the funeral had already happened. The turn is the timing, a message meant for the living reaching a world where its reader is already gone. What the letter said is left unsaid, for the reader to fill in.

`main.ts` writes exactly those six words to `/output/entry.txt`, validates the story is six words within 1 to 60 characters, and reads the file back to verify it matches byte for byte.

Built for the make.fun bounty "Six Words, One Story" ($5 prize pool).
